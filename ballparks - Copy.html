Quick Facts

30Major League parks across 27 metro areas — 29 in the U.S. and one in Canada (Toronto).
1912Fenway Park — the oldest ballpark in the majors. Wrigley Field (1914) is the only other one over 100.
37 ftThe Green Monster in left field at Fenway — the tallest outfield wall in the majors.
5,280 ftCoors Field's purple row of seats marks one mile above sea level. Thin air = more runs.
1992Camden Yards opened and kicked off the retro-ballpark era copied across the league.
PoolChase Field has a swimming pool past right-center — and was the first U.S. park with a retractable roof over natural grass.
Dodger StadiumThe largest capacity in MLB (~56,000) and the third-oldest park still in use (1962).
Temporary homesThe Athletics play in West Sacramento until Las Vegas is ready; the Rays are back at Tropicana Field for 2026.
