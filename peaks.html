Quick Facts

58Named peaks over 14,000 ft — 53 "ranked" (rise 300+ ft above the saddle to a higher neighbor) plus 5 unranked.
14,440 ftMount Elbert — highest point in Colorado and the entire Rocky Mountains.
Most in the Lower 48Colorado has more 14ers than any other state except Alaska.
Sawatch RangeHome to 15 of the 58 — the most of any range in the state.
1923Carl Blaurock and William Ervin became the first to finish all of Colorado's 14ers.
Pikes PeakThe view from its summit inspired Katharine Lee Bates to write "America the Beautiful" (1893).
Mount Blue SkyFormerly Mount Evans — renamed in 2023. One of two 14ers with a road to the top (Pikes is the other).
Start EarlySummer afternoon thunderstorms are the #1 hazard. Plan to be off the summit by noon.
