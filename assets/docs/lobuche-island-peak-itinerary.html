Hotels in Kathmandu

Going in · Kathmandu Guest House

2–5 Apr · 3 nights
Thamel's original guest house. Courtyard garden for meeting the team and sorting gear, luggage storage while you're on the mountain, and a short walk to the rental shops. About $40–$65 a night; guest rating 8.5/10.

You land at 06:35, so book the night of 1 April too if you want a guaranteed room on arrival.

Coming out · Aloft Kathmandu Thamel

22–26 Apr · up to 4 nights
Marriott property at the edge of Thamel. Hot water that doesn't run out, reliable power, a real mattress. $115 a night on Expedia for April 2027; guest rating 9.2/10.

Book a refundable rate: if the contingency days get used on the mountain, you'll need fewer nights here.

The Namas package includes a 5-star Kathmandu hotel for two nights before and one night after. Ask Namas to put you at KGH and Aloft for those nights instead, or credit them. If they won't, those nights come out of your pocket, which the high estimate assumes.

Tipping: Who, When and How Much

Tips aren't optional in Nepal's trekking economy. They're part of the pay, and for porters they can be a big share of what they take home for the season. Namas excludes them from the package price, so budget for them up front. These are per-climber amounts for a 20-day climb like this one.

Who Typical tip For this trip
Lead climbing guide (Sherpa) $10–15 a day on the trek, plus a summit bonus. Namas suggests about $300 after a successful summit. $470–555
Porter $5–10 a day. $100–200
Cook and kitchen crew at base and high camp About $5 a day, pooled. $50–100
Kathmandu drivers $2–5 per airport or hotel run. $10–20
Hotel bellhops and housekeeping NPR 100–200 (about $1–1.50) per bag or per night. $15–25
Teahouse staff NPR 100–200 a night, optional but appreciated. $15–30
Restaurants in Kathmandu 5–10% if the bill doesn't already include a service charge. $15–30
Total $675–960

How to tip:

  • At the end. Tip at the farewell dinner after the climb, not along the way.

  • Directly. Hand each person their own envelope, the porter included, rather than giving one lump sum to the guide or the agency. That way the full amount reaches the person who earned it.

  • In rupees. Bring clean notes and change money in Kathmandu before you fly to Lukla. Cash gets scarce above Namche.

  • With a thank-you. A few words in person matter as much as the money.

Weight: From Denver to the Trail

Your bags change hands four times on this trip: at the Turkish Airlines counter in Denver, at the hotel luggage room in Kathmandu, at the scale for the Lukla flight, and on a porter's back. The Lukla flight has the tightest limit, so pack for that one from the start.

Stage Limit What's in it Plan for
1. Denver to Kathmandu (Turkish Airlines) Checked: 23 kg / 50 lb per bag, usually 2 bags in economy to and from the US (check your fare). Cabin bag 8 kg / 17.6 lb, personal item 4 kg / 8.8 lb. One duffel: trek clothing, shells, gloves, liner, toiletries, plus a few days of city clothes. Wear trekking boots and the heaviest layers on the plane. Duffel 18–20 kg / 40–44 lb. Carry-on 6–8 kg / 13–18 lb.
2. Kathmandu: storage at the hotel Luggage room at the Kathmandu Guest House while you're on the mountain. Travel bag, city clothes, anything not needed on the mountain. Repack into the Namas duffel and add rented double boots (about 2.5 kg / 5.5 lb a pair). Leave 4–6 kg / 9–13 lb behind.
3. Flight to Lukla 15 kg / 33 lb total: 10 kg / 22 lb checked plus 5 kg / 11 lb hand. Anything over is billed by the kilo. Namas duffel plus daypack. Duffel 10–12 kg / 22–26 lb. Daypack about 5 kg / 11 lb.
4. On the trail: the porter Namas: up to 30 kg / 66 lb per climber. Your duffel. Porters often carry two climbers' bags tied together. 10–15 kg / 22–33 lb.
5. On the trail: you Your daypack, 35–45 L. 2 L of water, layers, rain shell, snacks, sun gear, first aid, camera. 5–7 kg / 11–15 lb.
6. Summit day What you can carry above 6,000 m. Harness, helmet and crampons on you. In the pack: water, down jacket, mitts, food. 4–6 kg / 9–13 lb in the pack.

The weight that matters most is the porter's. Every kilo in my duffel is a kilo someone else carries up to 5,000 meters, usually for about $20 to $28 a day. Packing light is good manners, and it keeps me under the Lukla limit.

Kathmandu buffer days

If the weather cooperates, you'll have up to four free days in Kathmandu at the end. Boudhanath Stupa, Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath and Patan Durbar Square are the big four. For the summit beer, the Yeti Tap Room is Kathmandu's craft-beer spot; save it until you're off the mountain.
